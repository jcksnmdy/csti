Description

In MiL, PiL and HIL the vehicle is the Simulink vehicle dynamics model, on a desktop or on the HIL bench, driven by the stack's own steering, braking and acceleration commands (sent over automotive Ethernet on the HIL bench, as on the car); its pose drives AURELION every step. In ViL it is the car's GNSS, IMU and wheel-speed estimate, placed on the test site and checked for quality.

Key behaviors

  • Same output in every stage, so nothing downstream knows which stage is running.

Stage variation

Simulink vehicle model in MiL, PiL and HIL; real state estimate in ViL.

Failure behavior

Poor or stale pose is flagged and stops injection.

  • Portable across nodes (supporting)
  • Injection latency limit (primary)
  • Virtual-actor placement accuracy (primary)

Related decisions

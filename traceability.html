3. Constraints to design

ConstraintWhere the design honors it
ROS 2 stack in one containerGateway on fusion's ROS 2 inputs (ROS 2 stack with C++ fusion core).
Stack not modifiedFrozen contract (Frozen stack contract) on fusion's existing topics (Injection on fusion's existing topics).
Generic hardware, public siteVehicle-specific values live in a vehicle profile, not on this site.
Closed course with safety driverCSTI has no safety role; the stack's own safety chain and the driver keep the vehicle safe (CSTI has no safety authority, Move ViL from a closed course to public roads).
Vehicle localization on ROS 2Read by the ego-state bridge.
No single vendor formatOpen scenario format (Simulator-agnostic scenario format) and adapters (Adapter pattern).

4. Completeness checks

  • Forward: 45 of 45 requirements trace to at least one component. Untraced: none.
  • Backward: 14 of 14 components trace to at least one requirement. Components with no requirement: none.
  • Decisions: all 14 decisions on the rationale page cite at least one requirement.

5. Gaps and weak spots

Requirement(s)Issue
New-user onboarding timeOnboarding time depends mostly on documentation, which no component owns.
Portable across nodesPortability is met by the runtime as a whole, not by one component.
Stop injection on stack fault or overrideDepends on the stack publishing its status promptly; that signal belongs to the stack.
CSTI cannot affect the stack's safety chainVerified by test on the HIL bench; the vehicle relies on the same isolation.
MCAP and CAN log recordingsDepends on outside log formats; a format change affects only recording.
